ErasmusGo

Privacy policy

Last updated 24 November 2026

This policy explains what ErasmusGo collects, why, and what you can do about it. ErasmusGo is an independent app made by Kevin Kralj, who publishes apps as Keti Studios. It is not run by the European Union or the European Commission.

Who is responsible

Kevin Kralj (Keti Studios, “we”), Slovenia, is the controller of your data. Contact us any time at info@ketistudios.com or in the app: Profile → Ideas & feedback.

What we collect

  • Personal account: username, password (stored only as a secure hash), the country you’re from, your birth year (to check you are 16 or older), country of residence and alert settings.
  • Optional: a recovery email, avatar, short bio and the countries you visited.
  • What you do in the app: saved projects and their status, quiz answers and points, chests and stamp cards, friends, and certificates you upload (only you can see them).
  • Messages between friends. They are deleted automatically 7 days after they are sent.
  • Stamp card photos and texts you submit. If approved, the card (with your username) is shown to all players in the album.
  • Organisation accounts: email address, organisation name, website, country and the calls you submit.
  • A push notification token, if you allow notifications.
  • Approximate location, only if you allow it, to suggest your country during setup. It is used on your phone and not stored by us.
  • Reports you send about other users or content.

Why we use it

To run your account and the features you use (contract), to keep the community safe and prevent abuse (legitimate interest), and to send notifications you turned on (consent). We do not sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps.

Who can see what

  • Friends see your username, avatar and bio, and the messages you send them.
  • Leaderboards: your username and points are shown to your friends, and on public leaderboards only if you switch that on.
  • Approved stamp cards show the photographer’s username to everyone.
  • Everything else (saved projects, certificates, recovery email, birth year) is private.

Services we use

  • Supabase (database, login and file storage), hosted in the EU (Frankfurt).
  • Expo, Google and Apple to deliver push notifications.
  • Resend, to send sign-in and recovery codes by email — only to organisation accounts and to people who add a recovery email.
  • Anthropic (an AI service) may tidy up the text of calls submitted by organisations. Personal messages are never sent to it.

How long we keep it

Messages: 7 days. Everything else: until you delete your account. Delete your account in Profile and all your data is removed, except approved stamp cards, which stay in the album without your name.

Your rights

You can see, correct, download or delete your data, object to how it is used, and complain to your national data protection authority. Write to us at info@ketistudios.com or through Profile → Ideas & feedback.

Age

ErasmusGo accounts are for people aged 16 and older. Without an account you can still browse projects.

Changes

If this policy changes in an important way, we will tell you in the app.
