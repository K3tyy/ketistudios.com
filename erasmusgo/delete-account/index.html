Delete your ErasmusGo account

Last updated 24 November 2026

You can delete your ErasmusGo account and its data at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  • Open ErasmusGo and go to the Profile tab.
  • Tap “Delete account” at the bottom and confirm.
  • Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app

Email info@ketistudios.com from your recovery email (or your organisation email) and write your username. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account, profile, recovery email, saved projects, quiz points, chests, friends, messages, certificates and alert settings.

What is kept

  • Approved stamp cards stay in the album without your name.
  • Project calls an organisation submitted stay listed, no longer linked to the account. Email info@ketistudios.com if you want them removed too.
